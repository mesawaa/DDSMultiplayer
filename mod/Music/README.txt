YOUR OWN MUSIC - music addon for DDS1 Co-op

Put mp3 or wav files in this folder. They join the game's music:
the street music of the city's areas and the music of La Ballena's club.

It is on as soon as the addon is installed. MOD OPTIONS (F9) has a MUSIC page:
- VOLUME OF YOUR SONGS: how loud your songs play.
- REPLACE THE GAME'S MUSIC (off at first): off, your songs play along with the
  game's; on, where you have songs only yours play.
- Every song plays in ALL AREAS, or only in the areas you pick under AREAS.
- RESCAN reads the folder again.

AN AREA'S OWN SONGS
Make a folder here named after an area and put songs in it: they play in that
area only (with the game's songs, or alone with REPLACE on). The names
(capitals do not matter):

  Old town        (also: West old town, East old town - the game has one music area for both)
  Two towers
  Stink
  Downtown
  Deadtown        (also: West deadtown, Mid deadtown, East deadtown - one music area)
  Bad asia
  Tower blocks
  Commune
  Fancy hill
  The club        (La Ballena's night club)

CO-OP
The host's songs (with the host's areas and folders) are sent to every guest
once and kept in the folder _from_host. Everybody in the club hears the same
song. Edwin, behind the club's bar, has a CUSTOM tab with the host's songs.
A guest's own songs play in the guest's own game only.

The songs follow the game's MUSIC volume too. The files are only read, never changed.
